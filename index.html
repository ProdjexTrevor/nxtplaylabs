The Grind Pass

A training app for baseball and softball teams. Coaches plan and assign the work. Players complete it, upload proof, and earn XP, streaks and rewards. The whole team sees it happen in the Clubhouse feed.

  • Daily plans from the coach, on the player's phone
  • Photo and video proof the coach reviews
  • XP, streaks, levels and rewards that keep players coming back

How we build

Made for the dugout, not the boardroom.

Coaches first

Fits how teams already work

Coaches assign, players do the work, coaches review it. We build around that loop instead of adding busywork.

Safe by default

Built for young athletes

Accounts start at 13. Profiles of players under 18 stay private to their team. Anyone can report or block, and coaches can remove posts. No ads, and we never sell data.

Every surface

One account, any screen

Players train from their phones. Coaches plan from a laptop or the app. Everything stays in sync.
