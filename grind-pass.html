The daily loop

Plan. Train. Prove. Earn. Team.

Every day on The Grind Pass runs the same five steps.

  1. Plan

    The coach assigns workouts or a weekly plan from the drill library.

  2. Train

    Players see today's work on their phone and get a reminder when it's time.

  3. Prove

    They log it, with a photo or video when the coach asks for proof.

  4. Earn

    XP, streaks and levels unlock badges and rewards along the season pass.

  5. Team

    Wins land in the Clubhouse feed, where teammates react and cheer.

For players

Your work, on the scoreboard.

For coaches

See who put in the work.

  • Assign one workout or a full weekly plan to the whole team, a position group or a few players
  • Start from a drill library with written steps and demo videos
  • Review photo and video proof and approve it in one tap
  • Team challenges, leaderboards and a season pass with your own rewards
  • Announcements, scheduled messages and reminders in the team inbox
  • Moderate the team feed: reported posts and comments come straight to you
  • Works on the web and in the same app your players use

Safety

A team space, not a social network.

13 and up

We ask for a birth date at sign-up and don't allow accounts for anyone under 13.

Private to the team

Profiles of players under 18 are private to their team unless a guardian approves otherwise.

Report and block

Anyone can report a post or comment, or block another user. The team's coaches are alerted right away.

No ads, no selling data and no cross-app tracking. Read the privacy policy.
