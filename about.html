About

Next play.

Every player hears it after a strikeout or an error: flush it, next play. That's the idea behind the studio. Progress comes from what you do next, and the work nobody watches counts the most.

The studio

A small studio for youth sports.

NXT Play Labs is a sports tech studio. We build software for young athletes and the coaches who train them, starting with baseball and softball.

Our first product is The Grind Pass, a training app that turns the work between practices into something players want to do and coaches can actually see.

We work closely with coaches and teams, ship in small steps, and keep what we build simple enough to use from the dugout.

What we believe

Four rules we build by.

Reps over hype

We reward the work: showing up, finishing the drill, keeping the streak. Not just the highlight.

Coaches stay in charge

Coaches decide what the team works on and what gets shared. The software follows their lead.

Kids deserve better software

No ads, no selling data, private profiles for minors and real tools for reporting and blocking.

Fun is a feature

XP, streaks and rewards exist because they make the next rep more likely. That's the whole point.
