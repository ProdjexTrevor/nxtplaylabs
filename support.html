Support

Help for players and coaches.

Answers to the questions we hear most about The Grind Pass. Still stuck? Email support@nxtplaylabs.com.

Accounts

How do I sign in?

Use the email and password you signed up with, in the app or at app.nxtplaylabs.com. It's the same account on every device.

I forgot my password.

Tap “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen, or go to app.nxtplaylabs.com/forgot-password. We'll email you a reset link.

Who can have an account?

The Grind Pass is for people 13 and older. We ask for a birth date when you sign up, or the first time you open the app if a coach invited you.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Coaches: Settings → Delete account. You can also do it on the web at app.nxtplaylabs.com/delete-account, or email support@nxtplaylabs.com from the address on your account.

Deleting removes your profile, training history, posts and uploads. Workouts and plans a coach made for the team stay with the team.

Teams

How do I join my team?

Your coach sends an invite link or gives you a team code. Open the link, or enter the code in the app, and you'll land on your team.

I'm on more than one team.

You can belong to several teams with one account and switch between them in the app.

How does a coach set up a team?

Email us to get your team set up. Once it's ready you can invite players and assistant coaches from the coach console or the app.

Training

Why do some workouts need a photo or video?

Coaches can ask for proof on any workout. Upload a photo or short video when you finish, and your coach reviews it.

How do XP and streaks work?

Finishing a workout earns XP. Training on back-to-back days builds your streak. XP moves you up levels in your team's season pass, which unlock badges and rewards.

Safety

How do I report a post or comment?

In the app, tap ••• on a post, or press and hold a comment, then choose Report. On the web, use the Report link. Your team's coaches are alerted right away.

How do I block someone?

Use the same menu and choose Block. You won't see their posts or comments anymore. You can unblock people from Profile → Settings.

Where are the privacy policy and terms?

Read the privacy policy and the terms of use.
